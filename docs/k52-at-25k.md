# K 52 at a 25 KB budget on `src720.png`: not reached

Status: **target NOT met.** Best measured at the 25 KB budget is **K 47.44**
(the strongest *actual* stream under 25,000 B) or **K 47.82** interpolated to
exactly 25,010 B — against a K 52 objective, a **+4.2 K gap at fixed bytes**
after ~145 distinct arms across 22 screens. Every arm and its disposition is
recorded below, together with **three real encoder bugs found and fixed** while
searching.

Provenance: `build-pub/av2enc` (2026-11-25), `src720_1f.y4m` (1280x720, 1
frame, yuv420p limited-range bt709 C420jpeg), CPU `--video --frames 1
--cpu-threads 8`, Apple M4. Streams and scores in
`~/Downloads/av2enc-src720/hk/`; tools `tools/hk_screen.py` (fixed-QP screen),
`tools/hk_rank.py` / `tools/hk_curve.py` (equal-byte ranking),
`tools/hk_eval.py` (land at a byte target).

Our best K-52 crossing, from the same equal-byte method over every arm measured

better than us"; it is 28 % of rate below our own K-52 point and 3.5 % below
AVM's.

### 1.1 Why no knob closes a 3.5 % gap to a mature encoder

K 52 at 25,000 B, holding **AVM's own** JOD 7.696 and BA 12.68, needs
**SS2 = +2.42**. AVM has SS2 -3.19 at that rate — short by 5.6 points — and its
SS2 slope above 32 kB is only 0.94 points/kB, so AVM itself reaches +2.42 only
at ~27,009 B. Reproducing AVM's *metrics* at 25 kB is therefore already beyond
AVM; beating them by ~1 K is not something a preset sweep can reach.

## 2. The arithmetic that blocks it

At 25,010 B ours is JOD 36.63 + BA 4.68 + SS2 6.50 = 47.82. Holding JOD and BA,
K 52 needs the SS2 term at 10.68, i.e. **SS2 = +14.1**.

| arm | bytes | JOD | BA | SS2 | K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ours | 25,010 | 7.335 | 12.99 | **-10.70** | 47.8 |
| AVM | 25,000 | 7.696 | 12.68 | -3.19 | 51.1 |
| ours at QP 180 | 34,601 | 7.675 | 11.71 | **+3.07** | 52.32 |

We never reach SS2 +14.1 anywhere on our own curve — not even at 34.6 kB
(+3.07). SS2 gains ~1.3 points per kB near this rate, so the 25-point shortfall
is worth ~+19 kB. AVM is ahead on **all three** axes, SS2 overwhelmingly:

| axis | ours @25,010 | AVM @25,000 | AVM edge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| JOD (higher better) | 7.335 | 7.696 | +0.361 |
| BA (lower better) | 12.99 | 12.68 | -0.31 |
| SS2 (higher better) | -10.70 | -3.19 | **+7.51** |

SS2 carries 7.5 of the 8.2 total, so it is the axis to attack, but we hold no
lead on the others to trade against.

## 4. Three real encoder bugs found and fixed

All three are the same class: an ablation switch that `apply_video_nightly`
(the `--fast-decode` expansion) honours and `apply_video_nightly_b` silently
ignored. Each made a whole family of arms measure a phantom "identical".

### 4.1 `--video-deblock-off` ignored under `--nightly-b`

`quality` RD preset sets `video_deblock = true`; `apply_video_nightly_b` never
read the off-switch. Before: byte-identical to baseline (24,240 B
`b59f64f7…`). After: 24,239 B `24b521ec…` (distinct). Measured effect
**K 47.21 vs 47.82** — deblocking earns its place, so this closes a lever
rather than opening one.

### 4.2 `--video-cdf-chain-off` ignored under `--nightly-b`

The CDF chain was set unconditionally. Before: byte-identical. After:
QP186 27,778 B vs 25,172 B (the reset image is 2.6 kB larger, as documented).

### 4.3 `--video-cdf-per-frame-qp` ignored under `--nightly-b`

Now mirrored exactly from `apply_video_nightly` (including the
`user_carry`/`user_reset` precedence). This unblocks the CDF-composition
constraint, but AQ/delta-Q still fail the frame-schedule check (§3).

### 4.4 Regression evidence for the three fixes

| check | result |
|---|---|
| best arm QP186 | 25,172 B `8f0b85d8…` (unchanged) |
| plain `--nightly-b` QP187 | 24,886 B `6e0f1c9c…` = the documented `dmin16_q187` pin |
| `basic_64x64_1f` `--frames 1 --qp 90` | `84686a3f…` |
| `basic_640x360_1f` `--frames 1 --qp 90` | `d9bfa9b9…` |

The last two reproduce **identically before and after** the fixes (verified by
stash/rebuild), so the change is inert on the Basic path. **They do not match
the AGENTS.md recorded pins `c9294fa4…` / `0d90c958…` — that mismatch is
pre-existing on this build and needs separate investigation.**

## 5. What reaching K 52 at 25 KB would need

Not a flag. In descending order of evidence:

1. **A coefficient-accuracy fix at fixed rate.** SS2 charges the 1-px error
   band (`docs/band-d.md`: local SS2 tracks it at r = -0.82); we keep ~1.8x
   AVM's high-frequency energy while correlating worse (0.51 vs 0.55) — the
   detail is present but misplaced. `docs/band-d.md` already closed the
   two-parameter band-D / band1 routes as unpaying ("**Verdict: stop**").
2. **~19 kB of rate efficiency** at equal JOD and SS2.
3. **A relaxed budget:** the same arm reaches **K 52.00 at ~33,250 B** and
   K 50.20 at 29,602 B (`docs/k50-720p.md`).

## 6. Harness traps hit this session

1. **A broken accept-probe reported six rejected tools as "ACCEPTED".** It
   grepped a truncated log instead of checking the exit status. Always check
   `rc`, then screen.
2. **Screens must bracket the byte budget.** Early screens ran QP 178-184
   (29-37 kB) and say nothing about 25 kB; `EFF_PERC_CSF` flips sign between
   those ranges.
3. **`min8` and the adaptive floor win at equal QP and lose at equal bytes.**
   At QP 186 `m8a32` scores 50.32 vs 47.92 — but at +10 kB; walked down to
   25 kB it collapses to 43.52. Only equal-byte comparison is meaningful.
4. **`screen.jsonl` reads can race the writer** and return empty; re-read.
5. **A single-QP "identical" is not proof of inertness** — three of the
   byte-identical results above were bugs, not inert knobs.
