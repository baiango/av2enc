# Reaching K 50 on the 720p still (`src720.png`)

Status: **target met on one picture.** K **50.20** at **29,602 B** (QP 184) with
the one-frame `--nightly-b` key, `dav2d --strict 1` decode-gated (exit 0).
Extends `docs/k-720p-levers.md`, which got K 47.43 at 25,010 B.

Provenance: `~/Downloads/src720.png` (1280x720) -> `src720_1f.y4m` (1 frame,
yuv420p, limited range, bt709). `build-pub/av2enc`, 2026-11-25, CPU
`--video --frames 1`, Apple M4. Streams and scores in
`~/Downloads/av2enc-src720/hk/`; tools `tools/hk_screen.py` (fixed-QP screen),
`tools/hk_rank.py` and `tools/hk_curve.py` (equal-byte ranking),
`tools/hk_eval.py` (land at a byte target), `tools/hk_720p.sh`.

## 0. Answer first

1. **K 50 crosses at ~29.4 kB**, not 25.0 kB: **29,391 B** for the recipe in
   §1 (interpolated between QP 185/28,311 B and QP 184/29,602 B). The QP 184
   point itself measures **K 50.20 at 29,602 B**, so no interpolation is
   needed to clear the target. At the 25,010 B target the same arm measures
   **~47.5**, so the target is a *rate* the lever set already reaches, not a
   new tool.
2. **The best recipe adds one flag to `docs/k-720p-levers.md`'s winner:**
   `--video-rdoq-scale 0.01`. Measured at QP 184 it is **29,602 B / K 50.20**
   against the lean winner's **29,611 B / K 49.47** — the same bytes for
   +0.73 K.
3. **The RDOQ flag works by switching OFF the QP-scaled lambda, not by making
   RDOQ finer.** `--video-rdoq-scale` sets `rdoq_sse_scale_explicit`, and
   `rdoq_sse_scale_at()` then returns the constant instead of
   `lambda_from_fit(step, kRdoqSseScaleFit)`. At this QP the fit resolves to
   ~0.014-0.021, so 0.02 and 0.01 behave nearly identically and both beat the
   QP-scaled default: the flag is a *de-tuning* of the coefficient trellis
   toward the 240p-calibrated constant.
4. **K is dominated by SSIMULACRA2 and JOD, and our BA is already better than
   AVM's.** At the same K, ours holds BA 11.57 and SS2 -4.19 against AVM's
   12.42 and -6.73 at 23,215 B (§2.0 now carries AVM's measured curve). AVM
   clears K 50 at ~23.3 kB against our 29,391 B: **~26 % more rate**, not an
   18 % figure an earlier revision quoted off QP171.

## 1. The recipe

```sh
AV2ENC_RD_DRIVER_MIN=16 build-pub/av2enc src720_1f.y4m -o out.ivf \
  --video --frames 1 --qp 184 --cpu-threads 8 --nightly-b \
  --video-gdf --video-uv-qp-offset -23 --video-rdoq-scale 0.01   # 29,602 B, K 50.20
```

scored stream.

### 1.1 The alternatives, at the same bytes

Three arms on one dense grid (QP 179-185), interpolated in bytes. All three
clear K 50; the RDOQ arms clear it ~880 B earlier than the lean one.

The three are within the ripple of each other except at the low end, where

stronger above 31 kB. **This is a band, not a clean win**: the RDOQ gain is
+0.6 to +0.7 K at 29.5-30 kB and nulls above 31.5 kB.

## 2. Why the target needed rate, not a new tool

K = `100*(0.50*JOD/10 + 0.25/(1+BA/3) + 0.25*(SS2+50)/150)`. At the 25 kB
winner (7.280 / 13.44 / -11.61) the single-axis requirement to reach 50 is:

| axis | winner | needed alone |
|---|---:|---:|
| JOD | 7.280 | 7.810 (+0.53) |
| BA | 13.44 | 7.410 (-6.03) |
| SS2 | -11.61 | +4.29 (**+15.9**) |

SS2 is the binding constraint and no filter reaches +15.9 points.

Decomposition at the two K-50 points:

| arm | bytes | JOD | BA | SS2 | K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

operating point is small, so the gap is rate efficiency, not a metric the
objective ignores. That is the "low-rate coding efficiency" conclusion of
`docs/detail-gap-720p.md`, restated against AVM's K-50 point.

### 2.1 It is not an under-spend or a filter problem

High-frequency energy on the luma plane, against the source's own:

| arm | bytes | PSNR-Y | HF energy kept | HF correlation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AVM QP171 | 24,204 | 25.644 | 30.0 % | **0.5506** |
| ours QP184 | 29,611 | 24.818 | 53.7 % | 0.4976 |
| ours QP183 | 31,005 | 24.928 | 55.4 % | 0.5083 |
| ours QP182 | 31,699 | 25.000 | 57.0 % | 0.5150 |

Every one of our arms keeps **~1.8x AVM's high-frequency energy** yet
correlates consistently worse, and the correlation only reaches AVM's level
after another ~4 kB. We are not deleting detail — we are placing it wrong. That
is a coefficient/quantiser-accuracy gap, which is why `--video-wiener` and a
finer RDOQ both failed to help (§3), and why `--video-tcq` (which does delete
HF, to 22.9 %) loses 3.9 K.

Both arms on the same dense grid (QP 179-185), interpolated in bytes
(`tools/hk_curve.py`):

|---:|---:|---:|---:|

The RDOQ arm wins everywhere in the 29-31.5 kB band where the K-50 point
lives and is null below 28.5 kB. Above ~31.5 kB the two converge (both reach
K 52.1-52.4 at QP 180), which is why an earlier reading of the *same* data at
31.5 kB showed the lean arm ahead by 0.25 — the ranking flips with the byte
target, and only the band that contains the K-50 crossing is decision-relevant.

Caveat: the gap is a band, not a clean win — it is +0.24 to +0.58 and nulls at
both ends. It is reported as the better arm *at K 50*, not as a general
improvement.

`--video-tcq` shows the failure mode cleanly: at its own QP it is 23 % smaller
and 3.9 K worse, and its HF energy halves — it is smoothing, which is exactly
what `docs/negative-results.md` recorded for this content.

## 4. Harness traps hit in this session

1. **A label reused across screens silently scored another arm's stream.**
   `hk_screen.py` cached by `(label, qp)` only, so `act8` in a second screen
   reused the QP-185 stream from the first and reported five gates as
   "byte-identical" when they had not been encoded at all. Fixed: the cache key
   is now a hash of `(label, env, flags)` and every row carries that tag;
   `hk_rank.py` keys on it too.
2. **`--video-gops 1` is byte-identical for a lone key** (`a5136473…` both ways),
   so it is a free unlock of the fast-decode carrier's validation. It does not
   make fast-decode competitive.
3. **`AV2ENC_RD_DRIVER_MIN=8` and `12` are byte-identical**, and
   `AV2ENC_RD_DRIVER_ACT` is byte-identical at every value 2..256 — both are
   no-ops on this picture, not levers.
